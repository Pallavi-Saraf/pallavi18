My research publications span stellar archaeology, high-resolution stellar spectroscopy, chemical abundance analysis of metal-poor and r-process-enhanced stars, neutron-capture nucleosynthesis, Galactic chemical evolution, and chemodynamical studies of the Milky Way, including machine-learning approaches to stellar population classification.
